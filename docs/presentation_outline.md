## Slide 2: The Business Problem — The Cost of Disconnected Silos
**Title:** Why Traditional Churn Management Leaves Revenue on the Table  

* **Core Takeaway:** Telecom operators lose millions in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) because Commercial Care and Network Engineering tackle churn in isolated silos.
* **Key Talking Points:**
  * **Commercial Care acts without Network Context:** Marketing and retention teams issue bill credits, speed upgrades, or discounts to appease frustrated customers—without fixing the physical network fault causing them to leave, and often subsidizing low-risk customers unnecessarily.
  * **Network Ops acts without Revenue Context:** Engineering and NOC teams track network alarms and service tickets, but lack visibility into **which equipment models or last-mile segments carry the highest subscriber churn risk and revenue exposure**.
  * **Frontline Agents Flying Blind:** Call center reps spend minutes jumping between disconnected billing and ticketing tools during live calls, leading to slow resolutions, unguarded discounts, and repeat complaints.
* **Suggested Visual:** A split "Broken Loop" diagram showing **Commercial Teams** (spending budget on discounts for broken lines) on one side and **Network Teams** (fixing alarms without revenue priority) on the other, resulting in **₹24.69 Cr ARR at Risk** across a 100,000-subscriber base.

## Slide 3: Understanding the True Drivers of Telecom Churn
**Title:** What Actually Drives Subscribers to Leave?  

* **Core Takeaway:** Customer churn is rarely random—it is predictable and heavily driven by measurable network, equipment, and billing friction points.
* **Key Talking Points:**
  * **Frequency of Service Faults:** Unplanned service interruptions rapidly erode household and business trust.
  * **Repeat Occurrence of Issues:** Customers who have to call back repeatedly for the same unresolved issue are at the highest risk of defecting—while generating costly repeat technician dispatches (**₹350 per repeat truck roll**).
  * **Total Network Downtime:** Extended restoration times (MTTR) across vulnerable last-mile fiber segments (e.g., aerial cable cuts or high-loss partner splices) accelerate cancellations.
  * **Equipment Performance & Billing Friction:** Underperforming in-home equipment (legacy ONT routers / broadband set-top boxes with chronic Wi-Fi drops) and sudden account barring or billing disputes act as immediate churn triggers.
* **Suggested Visual:** Four icon callout cards (*Fault Frequency*, *Repeat Complaints*, *Total Downtime*, *Equipment & Billing Performance*) paired with real demo telemetry highlights (e.g., legacy router models driving **55%–60% churn** vs. **<30%** on healthy models).

## Slide 4: Our Proactive Two-Step Retention Strategy
**Title:** From Reactive Discounts to Proactive Identification & Mitigation  

* **Core Takeaway:** Stopping churn profitably requires a disciplined two-step approach: **Identify** who is at risk and why, then **Mitigate** through the right mix of engineering action and targeted commercial offers.
* **Key Talking Points:**
  * **Step 1 — Proactive Identification & Risk Tiering:**
    * Continuously score the subscriber base and segment customers into **High-Risk**, **Medium-Risk**, and **Low-Risk** tiers before they call to cancel.
    * Pinpoint the exact root-cause drivers behind each customer's churn score (network outage, repeat complaints, equipment fault, low usage, or billing status).
  * **Step 2 — Dual-Track Mitigation:**
    * **Proactive Maintenance (Fix the Root Cause):** Trigger targeted engineering interventions—such as router swaps, remote firmware patches, or fiber hardening—to eliminate service pain points.
    * **Targeted Commercial Retention (Protect the Relationship):** Deliver tailored, margin-safe incentives and retention offers during service recovery—strictly reserved for truly at-risk customers.
* **Suggested Visual:** A 2-stage chevron workflow diagram: **Stage 1: Identify & Tier (High / Medium / Low Risk + Root Cause)** $\rightarrow$ **Stage 2: Mitigate (Track A: Proactive Network Maintenance | Track B: Targeted Commercial Offer)**.

## Slide 7: Empowering the Frontline — AI Copilot for Real-Time Care
**Title:** Turning 5-Minute Troubleshooting Calls into 30-Second Saves  

* **Core Takeaway:** When an impacted customer calls in—or is reached proactively—the AI Copilot equips the care agent with instant root-cause clarity and a margin-approved retention offer.
* **Key Talking Points:**
  * **Instant Risk & Root-Cause Diagnosis:** The agent enters a Customer ID and immediately sees their risk tier (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW`) alongside ranked churn drivers (e.g., *3 Unplanned Outages*, *Repeat Complaint*, or *Barred Account*).
  * **Root-Cause-Matched Offers:** Instead of generic discounts, the system recommends an action tailored to the specific problem:
    * *Network Outages:* Margin-capped bill credit + speed boost.
    * *Repeat Faults:* Priority 4-hour senior technician dispatch + rental rebate.
    * *Billing Hold:* Late-fee waiver + 3-month payment plan.
    * *Low Usage:* 30-day complimentary OTT entertainment bundle.
  * **Zero Margin Leakage + Ready-to-Read Script:** If a **Low-Risk** customer calls, the system blocks unnecessary financial discounts (`₹0` offer cost) while still generating a warm, empathetic appreciation script for the agent to read verbatim.
* **Suggested Visual:** Screenshot of **Tab 4 (Frontline Care Agent View)** showing a live subscriber lookup with color-coded Risk Driver badges, the Margin-Guarded Offer box, and the Care Agent Script.
